What the numbers are actually telling us
Look at feature importance: knee_angle (0.243) and head_hip_dist (0.195) dominate, while hip_velocity (0.102) and angular_velocity (0.098) — your only motion-based features — are the weakest. That's the core problem.
A fall is fundamentally a fast, sudden motion. A single static frame of someone lying on the floor looks identical to someone who lay down on purpose, or is doing floor exercises, or is just sitting low. Your features are mostly static-pose features (angle of knee, distance head-to-hip) snapshotted frame by frame, with weak temporal signal. That's almost certainly why recall is capped around 56% — the model can't tell "person is now horizontal" (ambiguous) from "person just rapidly became horizontal" (a fall) when motion features are underweighted and noisy.
