One thing to flag honestly: with only 56 training clips split 5 ways via GroupKFold, some folds may have very few or even zero FALL clips in validation, which can make recall_score undefined/unstable for some folds (you might see warnings about it). This is a real limitation of having a small dataset — not a bug — but if you see UndefinedMetricWarning or wildly unstable CV scores, that's why, and it's worth mentioning in your report as a data-volume constraint.
